## Design Context

### Users
The creator uses this small overlay while playing Farming Season and monitoring daily automation across accounts. They need to see what the helper did, why it waits and what happens next, without covering the game or repeating its HUD.

### Brand Personality
Calm, clear and practical. Keep the established dark farm-green theme and restrained pale-green accents. This is an unobtrusive control overlay rather than a separate financial dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction
Small readable Chinese text, stable alignment, concise status rows. The user explicitly wants modest font sizes, separate operation pages and a visibly distinct execution area. Preserve drag/collapse behavior. Do not use large hero statistics, repeated decorative cards or long labels squeezed against explanatory paragraphs.

### Design Principles
1. Show daily completion and actionable blockers first; put exact quotas behind one disclosure.
2. Remove duplicate balances, energy, payout ratio, NFT perks, credit balances and economic-rule prose already displayed in the game.
3. Distinguish account allowance from the shared order pool; never say coins are available when the shared pool is empty.
4. Keep title/navigation fixed and scroll the content; expanded details must not grow the overlay beyond the viewport.
5. Execution details and material counters are optional; current action, next step and errors remain visible and accessible.
